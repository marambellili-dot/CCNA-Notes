# Network Fundamentals

## What is a Network?

A network is a group of connected devices that can communicate and share resources.

## Why Networks Are Important?

- Share data
- Access the Internet
- Share printers and services
- Communication between devices

## Types of Networks

### LAN
Local Area Network used in homes, schools and companies.

### WAN
Wide Area Network that connects multiple LANs over long distances.

### Internet
The largest network in the world.

## Common Network Devices

- Router
- Switch
- Host
- Server

## What I Learned

- Devices need addresses to communicate.
- Networks can be local or global.
- Routers connect different networks.
- Switches connect devices inside a LAN.
